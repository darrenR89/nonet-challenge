How to play

Nonet is a daily sudoku challenge with a twist: it's a 3D cube. Nonet is the sudoku word for a group of nine, and every face of the cube is one. One new cube a day, the same for everyone.

Drag anywhere to rotate the cube.

Easy-day hint On Easy cubes, the back face is a copy of the front.

A new cube comes out at midnight Pacific time, and your first finished run counts. Scores add up on the weekly board, and each Monday the weekly top nine are posted. Play daily to build a streak, and team up with up to two friends in a Nonet Crew 連: your scores add up.

Pencil or Lock in. Pencil digits in as notes, or switch to Lock in when you're sure. A wrong lock shakes, won't stay, and costs 25 points.

★ Lock-first squares. Lock all three before any other square for +50 each, and a look at tomorrow's clues.

Blue squares. Each ring around the cube holds two lines of nine, and they share six squares, shown blue. So the three squares only the current line has hold the same three numbers as the three dashed squares only the other line has.
