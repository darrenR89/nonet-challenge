How to play

  • Stage 1, Dial in. The bold clues start twisted out of place. Tap a square and use the arrows on its edges to turn its row or column. A clue back home gets a short yellow bar. When the clues reach any arrangement that can be solved, the cube's grid flashes yellow and Fill in starts.
  • Stage 2, Fill in. Every face holds 1 to 9 once. Every ring crosses 4 faces, and two lines of 9 run along it, each across three faces in a row; each line holds 1 to 9 once.
  • Digits go in as pencil marks. Switch to Pen to lock one in. A wrong lock shakes, doesn't stay, and costs 25 points.
  • ★ Opening squares are bonus squares: your first moves. Lock each one before you lock any other square for +50. Once you lock a normal square, the rest are gone.
  • Tap a square to light up its two lines. If that line is finished, it fills pale yellow instead. While dialling in, so does a line whose clues are all home. Nothing is highlighted until you tap a square. Tap again where two lines overlap to switch.
  • Blue squares. Every ring of 12 squares holds two lines of 9, and both lines share the six squares on two opposite faces, tinted blue. Since each line holds 1 to 9 once, the three squares a line adds beyond the blue ones must hold the same three digits as the three that the other line adds on the opposite face, shown dashed. Use it to carry digits across the cube.
  • Drag anywhere to look around.

Scoring: Dial in up to 300 for turns (fewest turns ÷ turns used) and 200 for speed. Fill in up to 300 for speed and 200 for accuracy. +50 for each ★ opening square locked before any other square. +100 if you use no suggested turns. With no turns there is no Dial in, so only Fill in and the ★ squares score. Then × a multiplier for how far you had it turned, which grows faster on harder days, because fewer clues make a scramble harder to read. Easy: no turns ×1, 1 turn ×1.25, 2 turns ×1.5, 3 turns ×2. Medium: ×1, ×1.5, ×2, ×3. Hard: ×1, ×2, ×3, and no 3-turn option. Everyone gets the same cube each day; you choose how many turns it is scrambled, once, before you play, and it stays set until tomorrow. Practice is a guided walkthrough: one turn to undo, then a half-filled cube to finish, with no timer or score. Practice cubes never match a daily cube. Speed points are full at the target time and reach zero at three times it. A clean solve is fewest turns, target times and no misses.

Each day's cube has a difficulty set by how many clues it starts with: Easy (20 to 22) on Monday and Tuesday, Medium (16 to 19) on Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday, Hard (13 to 15) on Friday and Saturday. Every cube can be filled by finding squares where only one digit fits.

One cube a day, the same for everyone, new at midnight Pacific time. Your first finished run each day counts. Scores add up on the weekly board, and the top nine players each week are the Nine. Play on days in a row to build a streak. Lock all three ★ opening squares and you get a look at tomorrow's clues. During an event, your points also count on its board and, if it has sides, for the side you pick. Nonet is the sudoku word for a group of nine.
